Privacy Policy

Last updated September 22, 2026

The short version. On the free tier, OttoMiles never receives your location, your trips, or your earnings. All of it stays on your iPhone. If you sign in and subscribe to Pro, trip and earnings summaries sync to our servers so they follow you across devices. Raw GPS traces are never uploaded. We do not run analytics, we do not show ads, and we do not sell or share your data.

2. Free tier: everything stays on your device

OttoMiles measures your delivery and rideshare miles using your iPhone’s location and motion sensors, and lets you log blocks, earnings, and expenses. Without an account, all of that data is stored only on your device. We have no server that receives it, and no way to see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Two features on the free tier do contact third parties, and we want to be precise about them:

  • Map imagery. The location editor (used to set your home and station locations) shows a map served by Esri and OpenStreetMap. Loading it shows those providers the area you are viewing, along with your IP address. The pin you save is never sent to them; only the map tiles for the viewport are requested.
  • Address search. Address search is powered by Apple Maps: the address text you type, and a map location used to bias results toward your area, are sent to Apple to return matches.

Neither request carries your name, an account identifier, or your trip history. They are subject to Esri’s, OpenStreetMap’s, and Apple’s privacy policies respectively.

3. Your device backups

Trip and mileage data that OttoMiles stores on your device is included in your device’s own iCloud or computer backups, the same as your other app data. Those backups are held in your Apple account or on your computer, not by OttoMiles. We never receive them.

Detailed per-trip location logs are kept on the device for 30 days and then removed. Copies already inside an older backup are governed by that backup, not by us.

4. Accounts and OttoMiles Pro

You can create an account with an email address and password, an emailed sign-in link, Google, or Sign in with Apple. Accounts are provided through Google’s Firebase Authentication. We hold your email address and a random account identifier. We do not read your name or profile photo from Google or Apple.

Signing in does nothing by itself. With an active Pro subscription, the following syncs to our servers (Google Cloud, in the United States) so your records are available on every device you sign in on:

  • Blocks and trips: dates, times, platform, station, miles driven, pay, tips, stop count, notes, and the single farthest point reached on each trip. The raw GPS trace of a drive is never uploaded.
  • Expenses: amounts, categories, notes, and receipt photos you attach.
  • Settings: your display name, tax settings, vehicle, and the home and station locations you configured (including their coordinates).

Only you can read your data. Our access rules are keyed to your account identifier, and Google encrypts the data in transit and at rest.

5. Route insights (optional, Pro only)

Route insights is off by default. If you turn it on, OttoMiles uploads the location of each stop on a block (its coordinates and how long you dwelled there, without timestamps) so we can label it with a street address and nearby places. To do that, we send each stop’s coordinates, and nothing else, to Geoapify, a reverse-geocoding service. Geoapify does not receive your account identifier or any other information about you. Their handling of the request is described in Geoapify’s privacy policy.

The address that comes back is stored with your account. We also keep a cache of resolved addresses keyed only by coordinate, not by user, so a stop near a common location is not looked up twice. Turning route insights off stops new uploads; existing labels remain until you delete them or your account.

6. Purchases

Pro is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment and holds your billing details; we never see your card, your name, or your Apple ID. When you subscribe, our server receives a signed receipt from Apple that tells us which product you bought and when it expires, and we record that expiry against your account so the app knows you are entitled.

7. Diagnostics (both opt-in)

Crash reports

Crash reporting is off until you turn it on. When enabled, crashes are sent to Firebase Crashlytics with a stack trace, your device model, and iOS version. Crash reports never include location or trip data and are not linked to your account. Google keeps them for 90 days.

Feedback and logs

When you send feedback from inside the app, you choose what to attach. What’s included is shown to you before you send: your description, the screenshot (after your edits), tracking diagnostics (permission state, watcher state, last sample, recent arrival, cold-wake flag), and, if you tick “Attach recent logs,” recent app logs, which include your precise location history for recent drives (up to 30 days), sent only with that report. Every report also carries the app version, device info, and your account identifier. Your email is never included.

Reports are filed as issues in our private issue tracker (Linear), which only we can read; an attached screenshot is copied into the issue with the report. Reports may be summarized by an AI-assisted triage step (currently Anthropic’s Claude) so we can act on them quickly. Attached log bundles are automatically deleted from our storage after 30 days. Screenshots and the report itself are kept until you ask us to remove them, or you delete your account.

8. Protecting our services

To keep bots away from our servers, the app uses Firebase App Check. On iPhone this relies on Apple’s App Attest, which sends Apple a hardware-backed attestation that the app is genuine. It does not identify you.

9. What we don’t do

  • No analytics or usage-tracking SDKs. We do not know how often you open the app.
  • No advertising, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your personal data with anyone.
  • No use of your data to train models or build profiles.

10. Service providers

The companies that process data on our behalf, and for what:

  • Google (Firebase and Google Cloud): accounts, cloud storage for Pro, crash reports, App Check.
  • Apple: the App Store and subscriptions, Sign in with Apple, address search, App Attest.
  • Geoapify: reverse geocoding for route insights, if you turn it on.
  • Esri and OpenStreetMap: map imagery in the location editor.
  • Linear, GitHub (Microsoft), and Anthropic: feedback you choose to send.
  • Cloudflare and GitHub Pages: serve this website and route our email.

We share with each only what that job needs, as described above.

11. Retention and deletion

  • Account data (Section 4 and 5) is kept while your account exists. If your Pro subscription lapses, sync stops but your cloud copy is kept so re-subscribing picks up where you left off.
  • Feedback log bundles: deleted after 30 days. Crash reports: 90 days. Other feedback records: until you ask.
  • Per-trip location logs on your device: 30 days, then removed by the app.

To delete your account and everything we hold about you, email privacy@ottomiles.app from the address on your account. We will remove your Firebase account, all synced data, receipt photos, entitlement record, and feedback records (including their copies in our issue tracker) within 30 days and confirm by email. An in-app deletion option is coming. You can also ask for a copy of your data or a correction the same way.

12. Security and breaches

We use Google’s encryption in transit and at rest, per-user access rules, and no shared credentials. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as North Carolina and other applicable state laws require.

13. Children

OttoMiles is for adults who drive for gig platforms. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

14. This website

ottomiles.app is a static site served by GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Those providers see your IP address and request in their ordinary server logs.

15. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and, for material changes affecting account holders, tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
